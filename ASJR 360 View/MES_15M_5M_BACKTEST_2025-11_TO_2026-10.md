# ASJR 360 View V1 — MES historical signal replay (November 2025–October 2026)

**Run date:** 21 November 2026 (UK).  
**Instrument:** MES Micro E-mini S&P 500 futures; $5 per index point per contract.  
**Source:** IBKR 5-minute CSV files already in `three_candle/data/` and `three_candle/data/ibkr_futures_bridge/`.  
**Scope:** full months **2025-11 through 2026-10**, 12 downloaded files, **70,689 5-minute candles**. Contract history: MES202512 (Nov 2025), MES202603 (Dec–Feb), MES202606 (Mar–May), MES202609 (Jun–Aug), MES202612 (Sep–Oct).

**Headline: The approved V1 colour signal did NOT produce a profitable standalone MES strategy under these assumed execution rules.** This is a preliminary, independent historical replay—not a native TradingView Strategy Tester result, not proof that the chart indicator fails to compile, and not a backtest of Gann-based entries.

## Data checks
- Source columns: `Datetime_UTC, Datetime_UK, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume` (September/October columns appear in a different order and were mapped by header).
- Zero invalid OHLCV records, zero duplicate UTC timestamps within contract, **256 gaps greater than 5 minutes** (mostly expected trading-session/weekend/maintenance gaps but not each individually audited).
- **23,563 complete 15-minute candles** reconstructed from consecutive triplets of 5-minute candles; no incomplete triplets in these files.
- EMA/RSI/ATR indicators reset on contract change, avoiding artificial cross-contract jumps; they continue across months for the same contract. These are **unadjusted futures contract data** and are not claimed to be a stitched/back-adjusted continuous MES contract.
- The 15m signal engine reproduced the *mathematical rules* of `ASJR_360_View_V1.pine` on historical completed bars, including the original 12-zone OHLCV volume pressure proxy. It was independently implemented for the study; exact Pine runtime parity and alert timing remain unverified.

## Indicator conditions checked on every candle
- **Bull trend**: EMA9 > EMA21 with both slopes rising (bear = opposite).
- **Bull pressure**: original formula, at least 8 bullish price zones and estimated buy share >=60% (bear = at least 8 bearish zones and <=40% buy share). If the source's pressure construction is inactive, signal = grey.
- **Bull RSI**: RSI14 >=55, also rising vs previous bar (bear = <=45 and falling).
- **Green / red** only when all three match; otherwise **grey**.
- **RVOL5/RVOL20 excluded from trigger and trades** as agreed: reference only. EMA200 also reference only. **No Gann support/resistance condition** in this experiment.

15m historical distribution: **1,470 green bars, 995 red bars, 21,098 grey bars**; **915** transitions to green and **627** transitions to red. Pressure had a nonempty estimate on **18,889** bars. A transition is not necessarily an actionable trade.

## Execution assumptions (important)
1. A signal occurs when a completed bar's 360 state first changes to green or red from another state.
2. **Enter at next 15m/5m candle open**, only if data bars are consecutive. One position maximum per MES contract, no overlapping exposures.
3. Stop distance = **ATR14**, rounded to the nearest **0.25 MES point** (min 0.25), target = **2× stop**. Signal bar ATR only; no future data.
4. Exit when stop/target hits on **5-minute OHLC** (replay); if both hit on same 5m bar, pessimistically count **STOP FIRST**. If unresolved, exit after at most 16 bars, or before a known market data gap.
5. **RTH** variant: require the signal and entry in US **09:30–16:00 Eastern Time**, exit by session end. Other variant uses all available extended-session bars.
6. **1 MES = $5/point.** Gross P&L assumes specified bar-price fills. **Net P&L subtracts a hypothetical $5 round-trip per trade** for combined transaction cost/slippage; not a broker fee quote. No margin, taxes or fill probability model.
7. No Gann confluence, chart manual confirmation, spread or news filters; initial 5m/15m results are not a completed ASJR discretionary strategy validation.

## Fresh EMA crossover safeguard — recent cross required

To reflect the ASJR entry preference of waiting for an **actual EMA9/EMA21 cross**, an additional **trade filter** required a matching EMA9/EMA21 crossover within the preceding **4 completed chart bars** (includes signal bar). This did not change the indicator's green/red calculation.

A crossover is essential to the user's intended manual-entry process, but the recent-cross filter alone does **not** make these automated ATR-entry assumptions profitable.

| Rules | Trades | Win rate | Net P&L | Profit factor |

**Interpretation:** Pressure filters reduce signal volume compared with trend + RSI. In this specific RTH ATR-bracket experiment, adding RSI to trend + pressure did **not** show an incremental win-rate or profit-factor improvement. This does *not* establish RSI is useless, because the signals and accepted trades differ.

## Conclusion and next research step

**Do not use a green/red EMA21 colour change as an autonomous buy/sell order.** It is a *confirmation overlay*. The preliminary historical performance here is weak and negative under the explicit bracket and 4-hour exit model.

Test the **Gann support/resistance + structural confirmation + EMA crossover + pressure/RSI confluence** entry as a separate strategy with causal timing, risk-based stops, RTH/extended segmentation, costs, trade overlap limits, out-of-sample dates, and a transparent comparison to the same strategy **without** the added confirmation filters. Then independently verify the Pine output on MES 5m/15m charts.

**No change was made to the ASJR 360 V1 Pine Script or the older Volume Delta Pressure V2.**
