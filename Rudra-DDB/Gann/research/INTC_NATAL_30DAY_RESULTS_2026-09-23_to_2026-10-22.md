## Rules (specified for matching prospective research, not asserted as an authenticated original Gann trading signal)

Birth dates: Intel original corporation founded **1968-07-18**; IPO **1971-10-13**. Exact birth times unknown; assumed local noon for planetary reference (Moon and houses uncertain). At 10:00 each UK day, use Swiss Ephemeris geocentric tropical longitude for transiting Sun/Moon/Mercury/Venus/Mars/Jupiter/Saturn against IPO natal Sun/Mercury/Venus/Jupiter/Saturn and founding natal Sun. Closest aspect within 2° orb: sextile 60° or trine 120° = symbolic `STRONG` (translate experimentally to UP), 45°/90°/135°/180° = `WEAK` (translate experimentally to DOWN), else NEUTRAL. This astrological label is **not established W.D. Gann theory or scientific forecast**.

Price actual UP = today's regular-session close > previous regular-session close. DOWN = today close lower.

Gann level table: use previous regular-session LOW as rolling anchor (otherwise applying October 22 low to September would leak future data): `(sqrt(previous_low)+angle/180)^2`, with the locked grid and nearest levels above and below previous close. Touch if target falls inside today's regular-session high-low range or within 0.25% of boundary. Touch is **not** a trade profit, nor a verified reversal.
