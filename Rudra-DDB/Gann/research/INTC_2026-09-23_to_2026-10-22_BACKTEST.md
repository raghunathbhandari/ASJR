# Rudra-Gann | INTC previous 30 calendar days: retrospective test

**Research period:** 2026-09-23 through 2026-10-22 inclusive; **22 completed US trading sessions**. Additional 30-*trading-session* sensitivity check: 2026-09-11 through 2026-10-22.

**Important:** This report was calculated on 23 October 2026 **after** historical prices were known. None of these are forecasts issued in September. Retrospective formula validation does not establish genuine out-of-sample prediction accuracy. The genuine forward test is separate under `Rudra-DDB/Gann/daily/`.

## Sources and inputs

- 22-day OHLC (Sep 23 through Oct 22) from https://uk.investing.com/equities/intel-corp-historical-data : dates, open, high, low, close; regular session only. Source differs from other providers by $0.01 in the 22 October low: Investing reports $84.64; StockAnalysis shows $84.65. We use **84.64** throughout this report, consistent with today's locked anchor.
- Earlier reference days Sep 9–22 from https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intc/history/ .
- 10:00 UK daily planetary longitudes calculated from the Swiss Ephemeris (Moshier computation); geocentric, tropical ecliptic longitude, shortest angle, aspects `0,45,60,90,120,180` degrees with an exploratory ±3° orb. Ephemeris library: https://www.astro.com/swisseph/ .

## What is being tested

**Avoid look-ahead:** Holding today's *October 22* $84.64 low as anchor during September would use future information and be invalid. For this retrospective test, every past session is independently calculated at its hypothetical premarket timestamp using only the PREVIOUS completed session's LOW as that day's anchor; this is the same mathematical Square-of-Nine rotation formula, **not** the fixed-anchor 30-day forward experiment.

For each test day `t`:
- `anchor_t = low_(t-1)`
- `L_t(angle) = (sqrt(anchor_t) + angle/180)^2` (modern Square-of-Nine calculation convention; not independently authenticated as Gann's exact direction-forecast algorithm).
- Grid angles as locked in README: `-90,-67.5,-56.25,-45,-33.75,-22.5,-11.25,0,+11.25,+22.5,+33.75,+45,+56.25,+67.5,+90` degrees.
- Pick a **single nearest level strictly ABOVE** previous close, and a **single nearest level strictly BELOW** previous close as the day's two directional price targets. No fitting those levels to the day's actual candles.
- `% upper/lower = 100 * (target / previous_close - 1)`.
- A **level touch** means target lies within the actual session low-to-high range or within 0.25% of the nearest range boundary. This does **not** prove a rebound, reversal, executable trade or profit. Gap-through cases with no traded price at level count as non-touch.
- Control: identical touch definition for the previous day's actual HIGH and actual LOW.
- **Actual UP/DOWN outcome:** session CLOSE >/< previous CLOSE. The calculated Gann price grid does not, by itself, output UP or DOWN. Planetary aspects also have **no authenticated prior mapping to an INTC price direction**; hence astronomical directional forecast accuracy cannot honestly be calculated.
- **Supplementary non-Gann directional benchmark**: prior session's higher-high & higher-low = UP, lower-high & lower-low = DOWN, otherwise MIXED. It is plainly labelled a basic price-structure proxy, NOT a historically authenticated Gann planetary rule. Compare direction only in the 18 non-MIXED days.
- No intraday time-ordered evidence was used: reversal accuracy / trade win rate / maximum attainable profits are all **UNVERIFIED**, not successes.

| Previous-day high/low control touched on actual-close direction side | 12/22 (54.5%) |
| Non-Gann prior-day price-structure UP/DOWN proxy | 8 correct / 18 called = **44.4%** |
| Simple always-DOWN baseline for this bearish sample | 16/22 = **72.7%** |
| Planetary directional prediction accuracy | **NOT MEASURABLE**: no precommitted historically supported direction mapping |
| Intraday reversal and profit accuracy | **NOT VERIFIED** without ordered intraday OHLC / executable fills |

**Interpretation:** The first Gann target pair was touched on fewer days than the control previous-day high/low pair (13 vs 16). That is **not supporting evidence** for a predictive Gann edge. The seemingly excellent 'any of four levels' statistic is not a fair predictive test: checking more levels will mechanically increase hit probability. The price-structure proxy is weaker than the simple DOWN baseline in this period. Twenty-two highly correlated daily observations are far too few for a robust edge test.

## Supplement: 30 trading sessions

For 2026-09-11 through 2026-10-22, actual UP 11, DOWN 19; Gann nearest-upper and nearest-lower levels each touched 15/30, either first directional level touched 16/30 (53.3%), while the previous-high-or-low baseline touched 19/30 (63.3%). Supplementary price-structure direction calls were 12/25 correct (48.0%). This broadly agrees with the primary 30-calendar-day conclusion.

## Astro methodology / limitations

Ephemeris was calculated separately for each observed US trading date at 10:00 UK. Reported longitudes are astronomical geometry, not evidence of any causal price movement. The 22 sessions yielded 6–14 planetary pairs within ±3° of any one of the six preselected aspect angles each day. Because a Gann-sourced rule translating those aspect occurrences to INTC UP/DOWN was **not fixed beforehand**, any retrofitted accuracy claim would be invalid. Historical Gann 1x1 price/time angle also lacks a precommitted price-time scale and origin, so is NOT CALCULATED.

## Decision for future 30-day forward experiment

**Do not call target touch a prediction win.** Freeze levels *before* the session, separately track UP/DOWN and conditional percentage levels, archive misses and gaps, and use a fair previous-high/low benchmark. Preserve all initial snapshots and do not optimize on this retrospective result.
