## Changed in V2 (27 November 2026)

- **REMOVE the old Filter 2:** No top-5%-of-C1 candle-range condition on C2 low.
- **RSI threshold changed from below 30 to below 36:** Wilder RSI(14), read at the completed C1 close.
- **Retain Filter 1:** >=2% decline from highest high of 24 bars preceding C1 to C1 low (rolling-high approximation).

## Execution/interpretation limits

- The **old C2-low filter is completely absent** from this V2 backtest, removing that specific look-ahead issue.
- Intrabar sequencing of OHLC remains uncertain: existing stops checked first, entry-bar initial-stop ambiguity resolved stop-first, trailing stop activated after a +1% high, and a newly higher stop is considered hit in that same candle only when the later *close* is at/below it; this is a replay convention, not proof of tick-level fills.
- Fees, bid/ask spread, slippage, and extended-hours liquidity are not modeled.
- R is a 1%-of-entry price move, not a portfolio percentage. A one-day backtest is too small to validate win rate or profitability.
