V2 / V3 · Entry / exit logic

Trend: EMA5 falls at least 0.15% over three bars.
F1: Swing down at least 2% from the high of the preceding 24 bars to C1 low.
C1: Full high-to-low range below EMA5; RSI(14) <36 at C1 close.
V3 only: RSI(C1) > RSI(previous).
C2: Immediate next candle breaks BOTH C1 close +0.2% and C1 high +$0.02.
Risk: Initial stop −1%; after price reaches +1%, keep the full position and trail 0.2% below the best high.
Removed: C2 5%-of-C1-range pullback filter is OFF.
Day end: Any open position is marked out at the final observed close.
